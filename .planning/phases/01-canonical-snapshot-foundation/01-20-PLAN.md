## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Purge process -> PostgreSQL | Deletion owner must be least-privilege and independent of broker state. |
| Dispatch scheduler -> external Redis | A stalled external call must not share purge execution control. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-20-01 | Denial of Service | shared scheduler loop | mitigate | Separate long-running process, connection, stop event, and cadence. |
| T-01-20-02 | Information Disclosure | purge errors | mitigate | Safe fixed status logging; discard raw exception and evidence content. |
| T-01-20-03 | Repudiation | process restart | mitigate | Immediate startup purge and PostgreSQL-persisted last success. |
| T-01-20-04 | Elevation of Privilege | purge environment | mitigate | PostgreSQL-only runtime; no broker or Browserless configuration. |
</threat_model>

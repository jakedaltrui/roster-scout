<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries
- Redis delivers untrusted/repeated task messages to the worker.
- Worker failures cross into durable customer-visible job state.
- Failed NCAA HTML crosses into temporary operator-only evidence storage.

## STRIDE Register
| threat_id | category | component | severity | disposition | mitigation_plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-02 | Tampering / Repudiation | Idempotent report job | HIGH | mitigate | Unique active idempotency key, locked transitions, terminal short circuit, one job-to-snapshot result. |
| T-01-04 | Information Disclosure | Failure persistence/logging | HIGH | mitigate | Allowlisted failure categories, sanitized messages, no raw exception/secret persistence. |
| T-01-05 | Information Disclosure | Failed HTML evidence | MEDIUM | mitigate | Store only failed/blocked HTML, restrict path/API, expire after 30 days, retain metadata only. |
| T-01-06 | Information Disclosure | Celery message | MEDIUM | mitigate | Serialize one opaque job UUID; fetch all organization/report/source details from PostgreSQL. |
</threat_model>

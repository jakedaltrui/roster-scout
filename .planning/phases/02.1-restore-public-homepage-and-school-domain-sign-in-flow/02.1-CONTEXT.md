# Phase 02.1 Context

## Locked Product Decisions

- The original polished homepage and exact NCAA team selector are the primary interface.
- Visitors may browse and select a team anonymously.
- Authentication occurs only when generation/download is attempted or the saved-report portal is opened.
- Passwordless authentication resumes the exact selected-team request automatically.
- Active sessions suppress repeat sign-in prompts.
- Verified `.edu` institutional domains automatically create or join the school's organization.
- Institutional subdomains map to the root school domain unless an explicit exact-domain alias says otherwise.
- Non-`.edu`, malformed, and lookalike domains never auto-join.
- The portal is simple and minimal: saved reports, history, refresh/retry, and downloads.
- Organization administration is secondary and hidden from primary navigation.
- Existing secure sessions, tenant isolation, immutable snapshots, usage tracking, audit records, and sanitized errors remain.
- Pricing and billing are not implemented; future plan/subscription policy remains an open decision.
- Phase 3 remains blocked until this correction passes browser, visual, security, and regression verification.
- Production deployment requires separate user approval.

## Revised Customer Flow

1. Open `/` and use the original D1/D2/D3 exact-team selector.
2. Click **Download Roster Stat Sheet**.
3. If signed out, retain only bounded team/catalog identifiers and one opaque action UUID in session storage, then show the polished school-email sign-in handoff.
4. Complete hosted passwordless or approved federation with a verified school email.
5. Resolve the institutional organization, establish the secure session, return to `/?resume=download`, restore the team, and submit once.
6. Atomically accept the organization-owned report/job/usage/audit record, then build and download the compatibility workbook through the familiar homepage state machine.
7. Optionally open **Saved reports** to review report state, snapshots, refresh/retry, and exact historical downloads.

## Constraints

- No destructive Git reset or rollback of Phase 2 backend work.
- No local password implementation or email-verification cryptography.
- No untrusted request field may choose an organization.
- No raw email, domain, provider token, or resume payload in logs or audit rows.
- No production deployment or billable service provisioning.

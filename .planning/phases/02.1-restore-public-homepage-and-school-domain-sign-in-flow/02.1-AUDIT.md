# Corrective UI and Flow Audit

## Finding

The polished homepage was not removed. `templates/index.html` has no committed Phase 2 diff from the pre-Phase-2 baseline. The regression is architectural: the Phase 2 default-deny gate makes `/`, `/api/teams`, `/api/team-logo/*`, and `/scrape` authenticated product routes; authenticated `/` then redirects to `/reports`. The original page therefore exists but is no longer the product entry point.

## Before Phase 2

- `/` always rendered the polished Roster Scout landing page.
- The division selector, searchable exact-team picker, workbook preview, loading sequence, and direct download were one continuous experience.
- Team catalog and logo requests were public.
- The browser posted the selected team to `/scrape` and initiated the workbook download on the same page.

## Current Phase 2

- Anonymous `/` is intercepted and redirected to a generic sign-in card.
- Signed-in `/` redirects to the report library.
- Public team discovery cannot function because its catalog and logo endpoints are also behind the organization gate.
- The sign-in page has no selected-opponent context and no automatic continuation contract.
- The organization/report shell presents the portal as the primary product and exposes more administration than the core workflow requires.
- The security model explicitly forbids domain-based membership, which conflicts with the revised product direction.

## Corrective Direction

1. `/` is the primary public product surface for every visitor and signed-in user.
2. Exact team search remains public; report generation and workbook delivery require authentication.
3. The selected NCAA team is retained in bounded session storage when a 401 requires sign-in.
4. Hosted passwordless authentication returns to one exact allowlisted resume URL; the browser restores the selection and submits it once.
5. A successful homepage request is also accepted into the active organization report library before the compatibility workbook is built.
6. Verified institutional `.edu` identity becomes authorization input: the server maps the canonical institutional domain to one organization and membership.
7. The portal is a minimal secondary surface: saved reports, report detail/history, refresh, retry, and downloads. Organization administration remains available only through direct role-gated routes and is not primary navigation.

## Security Boundary Change

The old rule “domain may suggest but never grant membership” is superseded. The new rule is narrower and explicit:

- Only a provider-verified email may reach domain resolution.
- Automatic membership requires a syntactically valid ASCII/IDNA institutional domain whose public suffix is exactly `.edu`.
- The default tenant key is the registrable institutional root (`school.edu`), so `athletics.school.edu` and `school.edu` converge.
- An exact, operator-managed domain alias may override root mapping for a separately administered campus or school unit.
- Lookalikes such as `school.edu.example.com`, `school-edu.com`, malformed labels, and non-`.edu` domains never auto-join.
- Domain membership provenance is persisted. If the provider subject later verifies an address at a different institution, the prior domain-derived membership is deactivated before the new one is activated. Explicit invitation memberships are not silently rewritten.
- The first verified member of a newly created institutional organization is its administrator; later domain members are ordinary members. Administration is hidden from the core flow.

## Product Boundary

Pricing and billing remain undecided and out of scope. Organizations stay durable and subscription-ready by identity, but this correction adds no plan, entitlement, checkout, or payment behavior.

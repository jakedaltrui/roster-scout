# Phase 02.1 Security Review

**Verdict:** PASS for the corrective local release gate. Production launch remains blocked.

## Authorization boundary

- Organization membership is derived only from the identity provider's verified email address. Provider organization, role, and membership claims are ignored.
- The local resolver normalizes IDNA safely, requires a true `.edu` suffix, rejects malformed labels and suffix lookalikes, and folds ordinary school subdomains to the institutional root.
- An exact operator-managed `OrganizationDomain` alias takes precedence over root fallback. This supports exceptional school structures without trusting arbitrary subdomain text.
- PostgreSQL advisory locking serializes first-use domain claiming. The first verified member becomes the initial administrator; later same-school members receive ordinary membership.
- `membership_source` and `source_domain` make domain-derived access auditable. A verified school-email change deactivates obsolete domain access while preserving invitation-derived memberships.
- Cross-organization report, snapshot, refresh, and download access remains deny-by-default and is verified as a sanitized 404.

## Browser and request security

- The homepage and team catalog are intentionally public. Workbook generation remains protected by an authenticated session, exact catalog validation, and same-origin CSRF.
- Pending browser state contains only division, exact team identifiers, display name, and a timestamp. It is bounded to 512 bytes, expires after 15 minutes, and is consumed once.
- The only automatic callback destination is the fixed same-origin `/?resume=download`; user-controlled redirect targets are not accepted.
- Report/job acceptance commits before external collection or workbook generation. Its opaque download UUID is the idempotency key, preventing double acceptance during resume or retry.
- Workbook success is tracked only after the existing recent signed same-origin acknowledgement. Audit and error output remains sanitized.

## Negative cases verified

- Non-`.edu`, malformed, IDNA-invalid, and `school.edu.attacker.example` identities do not auto-join.
- `coach@school.edu` and `coach@athletics.school.edu` converge on the same school organization.
- Concurrent first use converges on one domain and organization.
- Email-domain transfer removes stale domain-derived access.
- A second school cannot enumerate or fetch the first school's reports or snapshots.
- Migration upgrade, downgrade, re-upgrade, and model-drift checks include revision `0022`.

## Residual and operational risks

- Root-domain fallback is intentionally broad for verified institutional `.edu` mail. Exceptional institutions sharing infrastructure require an explicit reviewed alias or future operator policy.
- The first verified member of a newly observed school becomes its administrator. Primary UI does not expose organization administration, but the direct protected routes remain for recovery and future operations.
- Live WorkOS email verification and managed production infrastructure were not contacted by this deterministic gate. They require a separate pre-production smoke test and explicit deployment approval.
- No billing or pricing authority exists in this phase.

No production data, identity provider, deployment, or customer account was touched.

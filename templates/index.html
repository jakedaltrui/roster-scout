Quick answers

Know what you’re downloading.

No account setup or manual copy-and-paste. Choose a team and Roster Scout builds the workbook.

What is included in the workbook?

Every download contains a Hitters worksheet and a Pitchers worksheet. Both include player identity fields, while each sheet adds the season statistics relevant to that group.

Which teams can I search?

The selector covers the current NCAA baseball catalog for Division I, Division II, and Division III teams.

What file type will I receive?

Roster Scout creates an .xlsx Excel workbook and names it for the selected team and download date.

What happens after I click download?

The app verifies the selected team, gathers the roster and season statistics, organizes the player records, and prepares the workbook before your browser starts the download.
